# Working together without wasting attention

For agents, experiences, connections and conversations. People can direct and inspect
collaboration without following every exchange, and meaningful results return.

**Example:** Agents prepare a recommendation in a quiet huddle. A person can join or
correct them; one useful answer returns with important disagreements and decisions.

## Commitments

- **Participate usefully.** Direct or addressed input merits a response as appropriate;
  ambient conversation may merit silence, with no public “nothing to add” or working
  indicator. Fresh attributed human input is not charged or suppressed by ambient
  quotas. Another addressee may warrant silence; actual service/access limits apply.
  Explain relevant blockers honestly.
- **Coordinate quietly and openly.** Reuse authorized conversations before creating
  huddles. People can inspect without prompting and join when useful. Access,
  participation, notification and work authority remain distinct. Support lively
  shared work when wanted. Reuse mechanisms, not another quota or classifier. Without
  a quiet destination, explain limits and offer a supported fallback; never flood
  the human channel or claim hidden coordination.
- **Own the return.** Nomination is not acceptance or delivery. Someone accepts
  reporting responsibility and retains the expected result, objections and reporting
  state in concise source-linked records. Return meaningful progress, changes,
  results and decisions to the original authorized destination. That duty needs no
  repeated permission and is not optional ambient news. Others add material
  corrections, not duplicate recaps. Use [observed outcomes](work-continuity.md#outcomes).
- **Separate activity, drafts and speech.** Indicators are temporary and scoped to
  destination and operation; one completion cannot clear another. Show a useful public
  operation label, Thinking… for model wait, or honest delivery/human wait; omit
  unsuitable indicators rather than spam. Append complete contributions and corrections
  in order without overwriting prior speech or reusing indicator timestamps. Chunks,
  pauses and failed streams do not establish complete answers. Keep private reasoning,
  arguments, credentials and other audiences' content private.
- **Filter control noise, not people.** Progress, approval controls, output echoes and
  connector notices are not fresh participant input. Filter by metadata and owned IDs
  before context limits, never prose matching. Keep notices visible; a person can
  reference their exact visible content without exposing private control data.
- **Keep controls accessible and agents recognizable.** UI, voice, CLI and authorized
  agents use the same operations and permission checks. Document programmatic access
  to navigation, selection, drafts, focus and view state; protect newer intent with
  revision checks. Count setup and administrator effort. Use agent names, they/them
  or it/its, not human genders. Distinguish agents from people. Shared portrait assets
  follow the [portrait convention](../reference/agent-portraits.md).

**Check:** Join, leave and correct a huddle; return its result. Test wrong-room late
replies, ambient limits, status-like human prose, parallel activity and failed posts.
Judge useful outcomes, interruptions and comprehension; check no duplicate speech,
accessible controls and honest fallback.

**Left open:** Layout, art, voice, notification preferences and coordination strategy.
[Composition](composition.md) owns source/audience identity. The
[ACP profile](profiles/acp-hosting.md) scopes attention and speech assembly machinery.
