# Continuing and recovering work

For boundaries accepting input or performing work. Correction and interruption
preserve useful progress and honest outcomes through advertised capabilities.

**Example:** A person corrects a briefing during research. It reaches the same work;
a lost connection does not repeat completed actions.

## Outcomes

Received, submitted, queued, incorporated, completed, delivered, independently verified
and requester-accepted are distinct. Incorporated means included in context, not
understood or fulfilled. A sender receipt cannot prove recipient possession:
requested, offered, deposited and received differ. Missing or conflicting evidence
stays unknown.

## Commitments

- **Preserve input meaning.** Follow [composition](composition.md) for source/audience.
  Retain revisions, original time and intake order; deduplicate source revisions, not
  equal text. Corrections do not rewrite prior evidence. Retrieved history, controls
  and own output are not fresh instructions. Cross-room input needs applicable policy.
- **Acknowledge durable ownership.** Accepted input has one accountable lifecycle,
  including completion/reconnect races. Reject unaccepted input explicitly when full;
  never silently drop acknowledged input. Retain source evidence until reconciled.
  An unknown cannot freeze unrelated authorized work or permit related uncertain replay.
- **Continue the same work.** Guidance preserves results, unchanged approvals and
  responsibility; it does not create tasks per message. Use supported steering or the
  next supported boundary. Account for admitted guidance before declaring completion,
  or retain it explicitly for subsequent work. Preserve public speech, limits and
  consumption. Exhaustion retains pending input and explains the stop. Busy conversations
  cannot renew tasks or starve others. [ACP hosting](profiles/acp-hosting.md) defines
  its specific execution model.
- **Keep effects accountable.** Advertised asynchronous work retains the same operation
  identity and authority for status and cancellation. Recheck grants and conditions
  before unstarted effects; Stop, retraction or failed dependencies prevent new steps.
  Settle started effects under their cancellation contract, retaining partial/unknown
  results and actual consumption. Serialize incompatible operations at the resource.
  Missing optional reports cannot erase effects or invent capacity waits; identify real
  review prerequisites. Valid work needs no repeated nudges; stalled or finished work
  does not justify empty loops.
- **Reconcile before retrying.** Disconnect, cancellation request, silence and maintenance
  acceptance are not settlement. Task, tool, model and transport clocks differ; a
  transport timeout does not redefine the work deadline. Recover existing identity,
  results, allowances and unknowns. Retry needs verified non-delivery or reconciliation
  under current authority, never blind resubmission.
- **Keep evidence honest.** Summaries cannot silently replace authoritative records or
  change unfinished work's meaning. Preserve source coverage and later input. Distinguish
  claims from verification; expose useful linkage/failures without private contents or
  credentials. Separate bytes, tokens, cache usage, cost, maintenance and latency;
  missing samples stay unknown. Compare matched outcomes before claiming improvement.

**Check:** Correct running and finishing work; duplicate input, lose acknowledgments,
disconnect, revoke access and Stop. Recover partial operations. Check actual effects,
order, audience, consumption and retrievable evidence, including optional-feature fallback.

**Left open:** Storage, queues, history format, transport and internal execution.
No universal inbox, compactor or atomic planning mechanism is required.
