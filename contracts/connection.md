# Connection

For integrations between agents and working environments. A Connection makes
supported participation, context and domain operations available through an
authorized bridge.

- Own the platform-specific setup, authorization, credential renewal and
  disconnection needed by the integration. Installable connections follow the
  scoped [installation agreement](installations.md).
- Supply attributed source facts and preserve source references through
  translation. [Composition](composition.md) governs identity, audience and access;
  [interaction](interaction.md) governs the shared participation behavior. When
  using optional shared attention, follow the single-decision responsibility in
  [ACP hosting](profiles/acp-hosting.md).
- Advertise available conversations, context, tools and controls. Domain resources
  retain action enforcement under [work authority](work-authority.md). Follow
  [work continuity](work-continuity.md) for delivery and uncertainty.

**Check:** Connect to a workspace or game server, exchange input, return a result,
lose an acknowledgment, and revoke access. Can both sides establish what crossed
and what remains unavailable or uncertain without inspecting private files?

Transport, packaging and the arrangement of routing, context and tools remain
open. A connection is a logical bridge, not a required separate process or a new
intermediary where a supported path already exists. A Minecraft connection may
provide world tools as well as conversation participation.
