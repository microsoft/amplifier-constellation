Where it fits

Connects existing ACP agents to Teams-owned chats and threads through its own authorized installation.

Outward roles: Connection. Supports: Conversation, Experience.

  • Teams Connector for Amplifier contains Tenant, chat and thread routing.
  • Teams Connector for Amplifier contains Jobs, delivery and session bindings.
  • Teams Connector for Amplifier contains Local setup and management.
  • Teams Connector for Amplifier integrates Teams platform and conversations.
  • Teams Connector for Amplifier uses ACP agent endpoint.

Requirements, map sources, and implementation details
  • Teams owns the source conversation and presentation; the connector does not provision a tenant, agent or callback host.
  • Bot identity, platform permissions and ACP engine accounts retain their separate owners.
  • No Tool conformance claim is inferred from administrative API routes.
